Barangay Science Fair Report

Ladies and gentlemen, welcome to this year's science fair.

The headmaster opened the program by thanking every volunteer. A freshman presented a project on mankind's use of plastic, while a congressman and the chairman of the parents' committee listened. A policeman and two firemen checked the safety of the venue, and the stewardess who volunteered as an usher guided visitors to their seats.

Each judge said that he or she looked for originality and solid workmanship. The fair needs more manpower, so every teacher is asked to bring his or her own helpers. The spokesman for the organizers reminded everyone that man-made waste harms the island, and that our forefathers cared for this land.

A craftsman built the display stands in forty man-hours. The cameraman recorded the event, the mailman delivered the invitations, the salesman from the cooperative donated supplies, and the waitress served snacks. A layman could follow every exhibit because the middleman who arranged the sponsors kept the booths manned all day.
